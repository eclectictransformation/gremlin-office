Client Waiver and Consent

Bryan Nolan, LMT · N.J. Lic. #18KT01214900 · EclecticTransformation.com · bryan@eclectictransformation.com · (973) 462-8810

Please read this short form, ask me anything that’s unclear, and sign before we begin.

Required acknowledgement

The following statement is required, word for word, by N.J.A.C. 13:37A-3.7. It must appear on the signed form before the first service.

Massage and bodywork therapy practices are designed to promote and maintain the health and well-being of the client. Massage and bodywork therapies do not include the diagnosis of illness, disease, impairment or disability. If I experience any pain or discomfort during this session, I will immediately inform the therapist so that the pressure and/or manipulations may be adjusted to my level of comfort. Because massage and bodywork therapy may be contraindicated due to certain medical conditions, I affirm that I have informed the therapist of all my known medical conditions and will keep the therapist updated as to any changes in my medical condition.

I have read and understand the statement above.

The session

I am receiving massage and bodywork from Bryan Nolan, LMT, doing business as Eclectic Transformation. Sessions may include Functional Massage (clothed) or Ooey Gooey Massage (spa-style, with draping). Massage is not a substitute for medical care. Bryan does not diagnose, treat or prescribe for illness, disease, impairment or disability. I will continue to see my own health-care providers as needed.

My health, my say

I will tell Bryan about conditions, surgeries, implants or hardware, medications, allergies and anything else that might affect a session, and I will update him if that changes. I can ask him to stop, change the pressure or skip any area at any time, for any reason.

Comfort and draping

In clothed sessions, I remain dressed. In spa-style sessions, only the area being worked is undraped; I will be draped at all other times. Intimate areas are not included. I will say if I want more coverage or a different approach.

Ordinary risks

I understand that massage can bring ordinary, foreseeable effects such as temporary soreness, lightheadedness or an emotional release, and I accept those ordinary risks.

Energetic restoration

Energetic restoration is offered as a form of faith healing — a spiritual practice by an ordained Universal Life Church minister, separate from licensed massage, with no medical claims and no promised results.

Occasional side effect: some people report feeling better. No extra charge. Results are not promised.

Other thoughts I may share

Any thoughts I share on diet, supplements, psychology or behaviors of the mind come from experience, research and work with people. They are offered for exploration only and do not replace a doctor or other medical professional.

Release

To the extent New Jersey law allows, I release and hold harmless Bryan Nolan, LMT, and Eclectic Transformation from claims arising out of ordinary negligence in connection with these services. This does not apply to gross negligence, recklessness or willful misconduct, and it does not waive any duty that cannot legally be waived, including duties under New Jersey massage and bodywork law.

Privacy

Your information is kept confidential, and records are kept as required by law. You may have a copy of this form.

I have read this form, had a chance to ask questions, and I sign it voluntarily.
