Ten frames, ten pins, real scoring. Nothing you do here is saved.

    How to bowl
    • Move: slide your finger sideways on the lane (or use the ◀ ▶ buttons) to step left or right.
    • Bowl: swipe up the lane. A longer swipe throws harder, and the angle of your swipe aims the ball.
    • Pull back: or drag down toward yourself and let go, like a slingshot.
    • Spin: curve your swipe and the ball hooks that way near the pins.
    • With a mouse, click and drag the same way. On a keyboard, arrows move and Space bowls straight.
