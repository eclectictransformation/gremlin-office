HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Find the red access card, open the red door, cross the server room, and beat the Mega-Copier in the Copy Center. Then hop on the green exit pad.

Touch (phone & iPad)

Tap & Drag (default): drag anywhere to look around. Tap the floor to walk there, and you'll find your own way around walls. Tap a monster or hold FIRE to shoot. Aim help and auto-fire do the fiddly parts.

Hold to Walk: hold the left half of the screen to walk forward. Slide your thumb sideways to sidestep, or down to back up. Drag on the right half to look.

Classic Sticks: a floating stick on the left, drag to look on the right.

Keyboard & mouse

WASD or arrow keys to move, mouse to look (click the game first), click to fire, 1/2/3 or the mouse wheel to swap gadgets, E to use, Esc to pause.

Gadgets

1 Sticky-Note Blaster: rapid fire, and the notes slowly refill. 2 Snap Spreader: a burst of rubber bands. 3 Confetti Cannon: a big party pop that hits everything nearby.

Psst

One wall in the cubicle farm has a note on it. Walk into it, or ping it.
